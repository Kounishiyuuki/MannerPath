MannerPath is a navigation and local-rule compliance utility for adults legally permitted to smoke. It does not sell or advertise tobacco, reward consumption or guarantee permission to smoke. Follow posted signs and local rules. No account, payment or sign-in is required.

Published coverage is partial: parts of Tokyo (Taito, Minato, Koto and Musashino), Osaka City and Kyoto City. Places outside published coverage show an honest empty state. Source, access uncertainty, freshness and approximate positions are shown. A destination search changes the browsing area, not the device location. From outside coverage, search for “浅草駅 東京” and select the Tokyo result to see published places. Distant destinations do not offer a walking detour; Apple Maps handoff remains separate.

Browsing uses When In Use location for nearby ranking, distance and bearing. Browsing requests to our service contain geographic tile IDs, not raw GPS. When submitting a place, a user explicitly chooses and confirms a map pin; this can be their current location. That pin is sent with the report. Report submission registers and uses Apple App Attest on a supported physical iPhone. Report content and private photos are linked to a device-derived identifier for abuse prevention; having no account does not mean the submissions are anonymous. See our privacy policy for retention and provider processing.

New-place proposals require 1–3 photos and consent to the approved report terms. Photos are private review evidence and never public images. Metadata is removed; faces and vehicle plates are not automatically redacted. A received report is pending review. Only approved facts may proceed through the separate publication checks. Submission or photo approval does not publish a place. If photo upload fails after acceptance, retry sends the remaining photos for the same report; after restarting, the user reselects the photos rather than resubmitting the report.

Reviewer steps:
1. Open the app, read the eligibility notice, and confirm. Location is optional for destination browsing. Allow When In Use when explicitly choosing to use the device location, or deny and continue with destination search.
2. Type “浅草駅 東京”, select the Tokyo destination, and inspect the published map/list. Select a place, open its details, and inspect source/license, access restrictions, freshness and precision. Use the directions action to hand off to Apple Maps. Clear the destination to return to the device area. Type/tobacco/access filters can be cleared.
3. Open Data & Privacy for privacy/support/contact links. After loading places, disconnect: cached place information remains available; offline walking routes are not provided.
4. To propose a real, unlisted smoking place, use the map/sheet add-place entry. Confirm the map pin, check duplicate suggestions, attach 1–3 safe photos showing actual smoking-place evidence, enter conditions (unknown is allowed), review the current terms, consent and send. Save the received report ID; the status is pending review. Do not create dummy reports. This needs the enabled service and real App Attest. If suitable evidence is unavailable, use the operator-provided recording of the same signed build and authorized flow.
5. The paired Apple Watch displays nearby data received from the iPhone, with freshness and cached offline states. Destination-search results are not synchronized to Watch. A real nearby session in a covered area is needed to demonstrate a populated Watch and widgets. Widgets show saved nearby data and update on the system's schedule.

Uninstalling or reinstalling does not delete server submissions. A report ID alone does not establish ownership for deletion or restore access to old reports. Contact support. There is no in-app deletion feature.

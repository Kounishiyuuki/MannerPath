MannerPath is a navigation and local-rule compliance utility for adults legally permitted to smoke. It does not sell or advertise tobacco, reward consumption or guarantee permission to smoke. Follow posted signs and local rules. No account, payment or sign-in is required.

Coverage is partial: parts of Tokyo (Taito, Minato, Koto and Musashino), Osaka City and Kyoto City. Other areas show an honest empty state. Access uncertainty, source, freshness and approximate positions are shown. From outside coverage, search for “浅草駅 東京” and select the Tokyo destination. This changes the browsing area, not your current location. Distant destinations do not offer a walking detour.

Version 1.0 accepts new-place proposals without photos. There is no photo attachment, upload or photo retry feature. Confirm a map pin, check duplicates, enter type/conditions and any explanation, read the terms, consent and send. Unknown values are allowed. A store or ashtray alone does not establish smoking permission. A received proposal is pending review. Approval requires an operator-recorded independent check, such as an official source or a site visit. Sending does not automatically publish a place or guarantee listing. Rights, location and quality checks still apply.

Browsing uses When In Use location for ranking, distance and bearing. Browsing requests contain geographic tile IDs, not raw GPS. Reports include the pin explicitly chosen and confirmed by the user; it may equal their current location. Submission uses Apple App Attest on a supported physical iPhone. Report text, pin and device-derived identifiers are collected and associated for review and abuse prevention. No account does not mean anonymous reports. Personal report fields are scheduled for minimization after 90 days; recovery history, authentication registration and minimal audit records are separate. See the privacy policy for approved retention and provider handling.

For v3 submissions, transient network/5xx failures may be retried automatically at most twice, using the same payload and idempotency key with a fresh App Attest challenge. No receipt is shown as accepted without a valid response. Refusals and rate limits are not automatically retried. After retries are exhausted or cancelled, input remains for manual retry. Restart recovery does not promise automatic background sending.

Reviewer steps:
1. Fresh install, confirm the eligibility notice. Allow When In Use when explicitly requesting location, or deny and use destination search.
2. Search “浅草駅 東京”, inspect map/list, select a place, open details/source/license, and use directions to open Apple Maps. Try destination clear and filters/clear.
3. Open Data & Privacy and support/contact links. Load data, disconnect and inspect cached information; offline walking routes are not provided.
4. On a physical iPhone, propose a real unlisted smoking location. Confirm the pin, check duplicates, enter details, read approved terms, consent and send once. No photo step is present. Save the report ID/pending-review state. Do not create dummy production reports. If suitable evidence is unavailable, use the operator-provided recording of this signed build's authorized flow. Simulator/mock success does not prove App Attest acceptance.
5. Watch shows nearby data received from iPhone, freshness and cached offline states. Destination-search results are not synchronized to Watch. Populated Watch/widget scenes require real nearby data in a covered area. Widgets update on the system's schedule.

Reinstalling does not delete server submissions or prove ownership of old reports. A report ID alone does not guarantee deletion or restoration. Contact support. No in-app deletion feature is claimed.

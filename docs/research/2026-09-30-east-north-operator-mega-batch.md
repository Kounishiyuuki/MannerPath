No approved source means no source-by-source new-spot list: **every candidate adds 0**.

## Method and approval boundary

Sequential requests with pauses; no aggressive same-host parallel fetch. A 403/429 stops that host. No proxy, user-agent substitution or certificate bypass. Search and third-party catalogs discover candidates only; findings below use publisher pages and downloaded original resources.

Decoded cells were inspected for 喫煙, 灰皿, smoking and F019, covering names, description, notes, facility genre/type and POI columns where available. Numeric POI inventories are retained in the audit; **no numeric value was established as a publisher-defined smoking category**. Zero text matches is a bounded result for these payloads, not a claim that the municipality has no smoking sites. No host/building/park coordinate, polygon centroid, address geocoding, Google/MapKit/OSM coordinate or image/PDF position is adopted.

Approval requires scoped original license applicability, redistribution, modification, attribution and share-alike review **and** supplied smoking Point geometry/CRS, current operation, scope/completeness, dates and stable fetch. Sources failing existence/coordinate gates are rejected without pretending their remaining licensing/current-operation review was completed. CC BY catalog labels alone do not approve sources. No unsupported values, observation dates or refresh targets are invented.

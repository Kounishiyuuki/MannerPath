## Summary

Reports are reviewed and may not be used. Only adopted place facts may become map data, labelled “MannerPath 利用者報告（審査済み）”, separately from official information.
Notes, observation dates, submission times, device-related values and contributor-identifying information are not published.
Personal report fields are designed to be minimized based on 90 days after receipt; this does not erase all records or recovery history. Removal requests follow section 8.

## 1. Information sent

A report includes its type/target, a selected map pin for a new or moved place, selected place/access/tobacco conditions,
optional notes and observation date, and the consented terms version. There is no account requirement.
Browsing uses current location on the device. A report sends the pin explicitly selected and confirmed by you; it may equal your current location.
Location history is not automatically collected. Names/contact details are not required. Version 1.0 provides no photo attachment.
Do not submit others' personal information or content copied from other services.
App Attest key identifiers/public keys support device authentication; key-derived submitter identifiers support review and abuse prevention.
Reports from the same installation can be linked; no account does not mean anonymous or unlinked.

## 2. Storage, review and processing

The operator stores, moderates and compares reports with existing data, other reports and other sources.
Approval of a new place requires independent official information or an operator's site visit. A store or ashtray alone does not establish smoking permission.
Normalization may round positions, standardize wording, structure selected fields and combine duplicates.

## 3. No guarantee of adoption

Reports are not official information. The operator may decline, partly adopt or later stop using a report after review, without an obligation to individually notify adoption.

## 4. Permission to use reviewed facts

If adopted after review, you permit the operator to use the resulting reviewed facts without charge and without a fixed duration:
adopted positions, existence/state/type/access/tobacco conditions, confirmation counts and review results.
This covers storage, normalization, structuring, combination, publication and redistribution through the app (including iPhone and Apple Watch),
API, map tiles, data distributions including migration bundles, and future formats. Section 8 governs correction/removal requests; statutory rights are not restricted.
Third-party reuse, redistribution and commercial use are permitted on condition of attribution “MannerPath 利用者報告（審査済み）” and the public terms URL.
Applicable laws and original-source rights/conditions remain applicable. This permission excludes notes and other free text, which are not published.
Many facts may not be copyright works; this clause also clarifies the intended use in that case.
Submit only what you actually confirmed and do not infringe others' rights, including by copying other services' maps or lists.

## 5. Information not published

Notes, observation dates, submission times, abuse-prevention values, authentication keys, device/app identifiers, contributor names or identifying information are not included in public app/API/tiles/data distributions.
Report IDs remain only in internal review-trace and migration records, not public attribution.

## 6. Retention

Based on 90 days after receipt, the design deletes/minimizes report text, pin, observation date, submitter linkage, free-text facility names and hours in the operational database.
The deadline alone does not complete processing; the operator manages execution and completion.
Receipt IDs, categorical fields, minimal review/idempotency/independent-check audit records and App Attest registrations remain separately.
Reviewed place facts may continue to be retained and published. Earlier information may remain in D1 recovery history/backups.
SQL minimization is not cryptographic key destruction or physical media erasure. Reinstalling does not delete server data.
Authentication registrations support device authentication/abuse prevention; receipt/review/independent-check records support processing and traceability.
These records currently have no uniform automatic deletion deadline. Continued retention and applicable legal obligations are reviewed individually.
Support emails/case records are separately managed to handle inquiries and document responses, not by the same automatic 90-day report process.

## 7. Attribution

Published reviewed facts are labelled “MannerPath 利用者報告（審査済み）”, separately from official/operator information. Contributor names and IDs are not displayed.

## 8. Removal and correction requests

Contact support for deletion, correction or restriction requests, including the report ID if known and details.
An ID helps locate information but is not proof of ownership. Do not email private keys, authentication data or identity documents.
Losing original authentication information after reinstalling may prevent ownership checks or recovery. Third-party privacy/rights concerns are investigated even when ownership is hard to verify.
Requests are considered under applicable law and confirmed facts. Personal report deletion and publication of independently confirmed place facts are separate.
Errors/rights infringements may lead to correction or removal; recalling every third-party distributed copy may be impossible.
There is no in-app deletion/history-recovery feature or automatic early/full erasure or withdrawal of already published review evidence.
For each request, the operator checks the target, authority, use and feasible processing, and explains the action or why it cannot be taken. This does not categorically refuse legally recognized requests.

## 9. Consent and changes

Before sending, the app shows a summary and full terms and asks for explicit consent. The consented version is recorded with the report and covered by App Attest.
New terms apply only to reports explicitly consented to that version; no retroactive application. Reports without consent or consented to draft terms are not a publication basis.
A client/server version mismatch requires an app update before sending.

## 10. Limitations

MannerPath information does not guarantee smoking permission or availability. Follow on-site signs and management instructions.
These terms do not exclude liabilities or user rights that applicable law does not allow to be restricted.
